Adidas US Sales Dashboard

This interactive dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of Adidas sales performance across the United States. It enables users to explore sales trends, profitability, product categories, geographic distribution, and sales channels. Key metrics such as Unit Sold, Total Sales, and Total Profit are presented alongside dynamic visualizations, allowing stakeholders to gain insights into product performance and regional sales patterns.

Key Features:

Product-level analysis with interactive filters
Sales distribution across U.S. states
Sales method comparison (Online, Outlet, In-store)
Monthly sales trend analysis
Key performance indicators (Unit Sold, Total Sales, Total Profit)
Dynamic product image visualization

Tools Used: Power BI, Power Query, DAX
